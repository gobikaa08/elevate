# ELEVATE EVENTS - Luxury Event Management Agency

## 1. Project Overview

ELEVATE EVENTS is a premier, high-luxury event management and experiential production agency website. The brand communicates creativity, luxury, professionalism, and trust, architecting world-class royal weddings, high-profile corporate galas, private soirées, and arena concerts.

**Tagline:** *"We Create Moments That Last Forever."*

## 2. Brand Identity & Design System

### Colour Palette:
- **Deep Charcoal:** `#151515` (Primary section backgrounds & panel structures)
- **Black:** `#0B0B0B` (Root body background & luxury contrast elements)
- **Champagne Gold:** `#D6B779` (Primary metallic accents, badges, icons, and CTA gradients `#D6B779` -> `#B8934B`)
- **Warm Ivory:** `#F8F5EF` (Primary typography headers & contrast text)
- **Soft Grey:** `#A7A7A7` / `#D4D4D4` (Body paragraph text & secondary accents)

### Typography:
- **Headings:** `Playfair Display`, serif
- **Body & Controls:** `Inter`, sans-serif

## 3. Key Website Sections & Pages

### Homepage (`index.html`)
1. **Hero Section:** Full-screen cinematic event visuals, luxury eyebrow badge, Playfair heading *"Creating Extraordinary Experiences."*, dual CTAs (*Explore Our Events* & *Plan Your Event*), and performance metrics strip (*850+ Iconic Events | 45+ Destinations | 99.8% Client Delight*).
2. **Brand Partners Strip:** Highlighting global trust across luxury brands, royal families, and iconic artists.
3. **About Section:** Rich narrative on experiential spatial design, bespoke floral architecture, and 14+ years of creative leadership.
4. **Signature Services (6 Cards):**
   - Wedding Planning (Royal & Destination)
   - Corporate Events (Global Galas & Summits)
   - Birthday Celebrations (Milestone Soirées)
   - Concerts & Live Shows (Arena & Festival Staging)
   - Private Parties (Exclusive Villa & Yacht Gatherings)
   - Destination Events (Lake Como, French Riviera, Bali, Swiss Alps)
5. **Portfolio / Archive Section:** Filterable editorial gallery with active filter buttons for *All*, *Weddings*, *Corporate Galas*, *Concerts & Shows*, and *Private Celebrations*.
6. **Upcoming Events:** Curated public and private ticketed galas with date badges, venues, and *"Inquire / Reserve Access"* buttons linking directly to the consultation form.
7. **Our Process:** Four Pillars of Perfection:
   - 01. Consultation & Vision Discovery
   - 02. Concept & 3D Spatial Design
   - 03. Planning & Master Coordination
   - 04. Flawless Event Execution
8. **Testimonials:** High-profile reviews from royal couples, Fortune 100 executives, and celebrity hosts with 5-star badges.
9. **Private Consultation Enquiry:** Interactive consultation form with validation for name, email, phone, event category, event date, budget tier, and custom vision notes, accompanied by luxury Champagne Gold toast feedback.
10. **Footer:** ELEVATE EVENTS luxury monogram and wordmark logo, tagline, multi-office contacts (New York, London, Paris, Dubai, Milan), social links, and journal subscription.

### Secondary Pages
- **`about.html`:** Detailed company history, creative philosophy, core pillars, and creative directors profile.
- **`service.html`:** Comprehensive catalogue of all event disciplines with feature lists and consultation triggers.
- **`service-details.html`:** Deep-dive case study into luxury wedding architecture and bridal concierge services.
- **`project.html`:** Full portfolio gallery of international events with category filtering.
- **`project-details.html`:** Case study of *"The Royal Lake Como Celebration"* with venue details and schedule overview.
- **`pricing.html`:** Investment collections (*The Soirée Collection*, *The Signature Gala*, *The Royal Grandeur*).
- **`contact.html`:** VIP inquiry form and global office directories with non-disclosure assurances.
- **`faq.html`:** Comprehensive advisory on booking lead times, NDAs, 3D renders, and international logistics.
- **`team.html` & `team-details.html`:** Profiles of our Creative Directors, Floral Architects, and Master Showcallers.
- **`news.html` & `news-details.html`:** The Event Journal covering luxury event staging, lighting temperature, and destination guides.
- **`404.html`:** Luxury custom error page guiding guests back to active collections.

## 4. How to Run and View the Project

This is a pure static client-side project with zero build step required:
1. **Direct Double-Click:** Open `buyer-file/index.html` in any web browser.
2. **VS Code Live Server:** Open the workspace folder in VS Code, right-click `buyer-file/index.html`, and choose **Open with Live Server**.
3. **Local Web Server (Node.js):**
   ```bash
   npx serve buyer-file
   ```
