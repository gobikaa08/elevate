Transforming Historical Villas into an Ethereal Realm

Commissioned by an international royal family, Elevate Events was tasked with engineering a private 3-day wedding celebration spanning three historic villas on Lake Como. The brief called for complete discretion, classical European grandeur, and continuous surprises over 72 hours.

Our production team orchestrated a fleet of 24 vintage mahogany Riva watercraft for guest transfers, built a floating glass ceremony pavilion over the lake, and sculpted a conservatory with 12,000 hand-selected white roses and olive branches imported from Tuscany.

The Grand Finale

As midnight arrived on the final night, guests were ushered to the lakeside terrace where a 60-piece philharmonic orchestra performed an original overture scored specifically for the couple, synchronized to a 10-minute pyrotechnic ballet launching from floating lake barges.
